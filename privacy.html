Privacy policy

How we look after your data

A plain-English summary of what personal data we collect through this website, why, and how you can ask about or delete it.

Who we are: SE London & West Kent Kids Club is a small, home-based childminding and weekend kids club service based in Swanley, Kent. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email us at thekidsclubhub@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry through the form on our Contact page, we collect:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your child's age
  • Your preferred days (Saturday and/or Sunday)
  • Anything else you choose to tell us in the message field

We don't use cookies or tracking analytics on this website, so we don't collect any other personal data as you browse.

Why we collect it

We only collect this information to respond to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to arrange and manage your child's booking with us — for example, confirming availability, planning sessions, and being able to contact you about your child's care.

How it's used and stored

Enquiry form submissions are sent to our inbox using Formspree, a third-party form processing service, purely as the mechanism for delivering your message to us by email. We don't use your data for marketing, and we don't sell or share it with any other third parties. We keep enquiry and booking details only for as long as needed to respond to you or, if you become a client, for as long as your child is in our care plus a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what personal data we hold about you or your child, ask us to correct anything that's inaccurate, or ask us to delete it. Just email thekidsclubhub@gmail.com and we'll get back to you within a reasonable time. If you've submitted an enquiry and no longer wish us to hold your details, we'll delete them on request unless we're required to keep them for a legitimate reason (such as an active booking).

Changes to this policy

If we ever change what data we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page. This policy was last updated in July 2026.

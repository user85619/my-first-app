Fees

Simple, transparent pricing

Choose whichever option works best for your family. Both include meals and the option of early or late care.

Option 1 · Standard Day Rate

£60/ 10hr day
  • Standard 10-hour day: £60
  • Early drop-off (6am–8am): £10/hour
  • Late pickup (6pm–10pm): £10/hour
  • 3 meals included free of charge
Weekend rate: £50/day when you book both Saturday & Sunday across the year, or £60/day when booking only one of Saturday or Sunday.
Enquire about this option

Option 2 · Flexible Hourly Rate

£8/ hour
  • Flexible hours, 8am–6pm: £8/hour
  • Early & late hours (6am–8am and 6pm–10pm): £10/hour
  • Bookable in advance, or last-minute if space allows
  • Meals included

Mealtimes

Breakfast is served at 9am, lunch at 12pm, and dinner at 4pm. Snacks are provided during any early or late hours.

About us

Childcare rooted in our local community

We started SE London & West Kent Kids Club because every family deserves childcare that feels personal, safe and genuinely fun — run by people who know the local schools and community.

Our approach

Play-based, child-led, always caring

We believe children learn best when they're having fun. Our team creates a relaxed, welcoming environment where every child feels seen and supported.

Individual attention

Small group sizes and consistent key staff mean we get to know every child and their needs.

Play-based learning

Structured activities balanced with free play help build confidence, creativity and social skills.

Fresh air & outdoor time

Whenever we can, we get outside — local parks, sports and nature-based activities are a big part of club life.

Healthy, happy habits

Nutritious snacks, kindness, and encouragement are part of every session, big or small.

Safety & qualifications

Your child's safety is our first priority

We hold ourselves to the highest standards of childcare practice, and we're always happy to share our policies and documentation with parents.

Ask us anything

Ofsted registered

We operate under full Ofsted registration and follow the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) welfare requirements.

Enhanced DBS-checked staff

Every team member undergoes an enhanced DBS check and reference screening before working with our children.

Paediatric first-aid trained

All staff hold current paediatric first-aid certification and complete regular safeguarding refresher training.

Low staff-to-child ratios

We maintain generous ratios (as low as 1:8) so every child gets the attention they deserve.

Full public liability insurance

We're fully insured, with clear safeguarding, medication and emergency policies available on request.

About us

Childcare rooted in our local community

We started SE London & West Kent Kids Club because every family deserves childcare that feels personal, safe and genuinely fun. Based in Swanley and looking after children since 2018, we know the local community well.

Our approach

Play-based, child-led, always caring

We believe children learn best when they're having fun. We create a relaxed, welcoming environment where every child feels seen and supported.

Individual attention

A small group size and a consistent, familiar carer mean we get to know every child and their needs.

Play-based learning

Structured activities balanced with free play help build confidence, creativity and social skills.

Fresh air & outdoor time

Whenever we can, we get outside — local parks, sports and nature-based activities are a big part of club life.

Healthy, happy habits

Nutritious meals, kindness, and encouragement are part of every session, big or small.

Safety & qualifications

Your child's safety is our first priority

We hold ourselves to the highest standards of childcare practice, and we're always happy to share our policies and documentation with parents.

Ask us anything

Enhanced DBS-checked

We undergo an enhanced DBS check and reference screening before working with any children.

Paediatric first-aid trained

We hold current paediatric first-aid certification and complete regular safeguarding refresher training.

Low child-to-staff ratios

We maintain a maximum ratio of 1:6 so every child gets the attention they deserve.

Full public liability insurance

We're fully insured, with clear safeguarding, medication and emergency policies available on request.

Want to know more?

We're always happy to answer questions about our approach, policies or availability.

What we offer

Care built around your family

From weekend kids club sessions to flexible childminding, every visit is designed to feel like a home away from home.

Qualified & DBS-checked

Our childminding practice is run by a qualified, DBS-checked professional with paediatric first-aid training.

A safe, nurturing space

A maximum ratio of 1:6 and a consistent, familiar carer mean your child is known, cared for and comfortable.

Fun, active play

Arts and crafts, sports, outdoor play and themed activity sessions keep every visit fresh and exciting.
